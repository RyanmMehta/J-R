Entry 01 · 2026 to now

MD Anderson Cancer Center and LBJ Hospital · with Dr. Hilary Ma

I'm adapting an MD Anderson questionnaire on goal-concordant care so LBJ Hospital can use it for quality improvement, and reviewing the literature on prognosis awareness.

Care that matches the goals a patient says matter most to them. The questionnaire asks patients about those goals so the care team can compare them with the plan.

Entry 02 · 2026 to now

UTHealth Houston, Anesthesiology, Critical Care and Pain Medicine · with Dr. Jamal Hasoon

I made the figures and drafted the diagnostic practices results for a national survey on interventional pain care. I co-authored 2 papers in Orthopedic Reviews: a case series on cocaine-positive urine drug screens and a study of telemedicine use after COVID-19.

A detailed report on a small group of patients, here 4, who share a clinical situation. It records how each case was handled so other clinicians can learn from it.

Entry 03 · 2024 to 2025

Freshman Research Initiative, Biomanufacturing Lab · with BioMADE

We extracted phycocyanin and phycoerythrin, 2 pigment-proteins from algae, and worked on their yield and colorfastness for textile dyes. I used freeze-thaw extraction, ammonium sulfate fractionation and SDS-PAGE, and presented the results as a poster.

Phycocyaninλmax ≈ 620 nm

Phycoerythrinλmax ≈ 565 nm

The accent colors in this journal come from these 2 proteins. SDS-PAGE, a gel that sorts proteins by size, showed how pure each extract was.

Entry 04 · 2023

Welch Summer Scholar Program

I studied how mutations at the U1C binding site of U1 snRNA change U1 snRNP formation and spliceosome assembly. The project won its category at UT's Fall Undergraduate Research Symposium in 2024.

Small nuclear ribonucleoprotein: a short RNA bound to proteins. U1 snRNP starts splicing by finding the place where a non-coding stretch of messenger RNA begins.

Entry 05 · 2020 to 2023

Summer STEM Institute

In high school I built a multiple linear regression model in Python to predict COVID-19 rates by county. The paper was published in the International Journal of High School Research.
