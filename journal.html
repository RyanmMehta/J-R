Chapter I

Research

My research started with a regression model in high school and moved through RNA biology and protein purification to clinical work in pain medicine and cancer care. Hover over or tap a title to see the paper or poster.

5 entries  ·  2020 to now

  1. Entry 012026 to now

    MD Anderson Cancer Center and LBJ Hospital, Houston  ·  with Dr. Hilary Ma

    I'm adapting an MD Anderson questionnaire on goal-concordant care so LBJ Hospital can use it for quality improvement. I'm also reading the literature on how well patients understand their prognosis and how often their care drifts from the goals they set.

    Care that matches the goals a patient says matter most to them. The questionnaire asks patients about those goals so the care team can compare them with the plan.

  2. Entry 022026 to now

    UTHealth Houston, Department of Anesthesiology, Critical Care and Pain Medicine  ·  with Dr. Jamal Hasoon

    I made the figures and drafted the diagnostic practices results for a national survey on interventional pain care. I also co-authored 2 papers in Orthopedic Reviews this fall. One is a 4-patient case series on cocaine-positive urine drug screens in patients on chronic opioid therapy. The other looks back at how a chronic pain practice used telemedicine after COVID-19.

    A detailed report on a small group of patients, here 4, who share a clinical situation. It records how each case was handled so other clinicians can learn from it.

  3. Entry 032024 to 2025

    Freshman Research Initiative, Biomanufacturing Lab  ·  with BioMADE

    We extracted phycocyanin and phycoerythrin, 2 colored pigment-proteins from algae, and worked on raising their yield and keeping their color from fading so they could be used as textile dyes. I used freeze-thaw extraction, ammonium sulfate fractionation, and SDS-PAGE to check purity and yield, then presented the results as a poster. In the same lab I built bacterial systems that express and secrete proteins from plasmid vectors.

    Phycocyaninblue, λmax ≈ 620 nm
    Phycoerythrinpink, λmax ≈ 565 nm

    The accent colors on this site come from these 2 proteins. SDS-PAGE, a gel that sorts proteins by size, showed how pure each extract was. Cleaner bands meant a purer pigment.

    poke me

  4. Entry 042023

    Welch Summer Scholar Program

    I studied how mutations at the U1C binding site of U1 snRNA change the formation of mature U1 snRNP and the assembly of the spliceosome. The project won the Gene Expression and Regulation category at UT's Fall Undergraduate Research Symposium in 2024. I also presented it at the ReSULTS Conference and the Kappa Rho Research Symposium.

    Small nuclear ribonucleoprotein: a short RNA bound to proteins. U1 snRNP starts splicing by finding the place where a non-coding stretch of messenger RNA begins.

  5. Entry 052020 to 2023

    Summer STEM Institute

    In high school I built a multiple linear regression model in Python to predict COVID-19 rates by county from socioeconomic, demographic, and chronic disease data. The paper was published in the International Journal of High School Research.
